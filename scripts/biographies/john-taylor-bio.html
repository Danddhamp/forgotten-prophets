Summary

John Taylor was born November 1, 1808 in Westmorland, England. He converted in 1836 (28) with his wife, Leonora Taylor. He was converted by Parley P. Pratt. He was ordained an Apostle in 1838 (30) after moving to Farwest, MO. John Taylor was present when Joseph Smith Jr. and Hyrum Smith were martyred in 1844(36). He sustained Brigham Young as Joseph Smith's successor and aided in migrations west. In 1875 (67) John Taylor was sustained as the President of the Council of the Twelve Apostles. In 1880 (72) John Taylor was sustained as the Third President of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. He died in 1887 (79) from heart failure.
