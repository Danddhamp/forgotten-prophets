Matthias F. Cowley

1858-1940

Ordained an Apostle: October 7, 1897

Resigned from the Apostleship: October 28, 1905

Priesthood Suspended: May 11, 1911

Priesthood Restored: April 3, 1936

Biography

Matthias F. Cowley was born on the 25th of August, 1858 in Salt Lake City, Utah Territory, United States of America. He was born to parents, Matthias Cowley(Father) and Sarah E. Foss(Mother).

Matthias F. Cowley was baptised at the age of 8, in 1866, by Samuel Turnbow.

Matthias served two missions in the Southern States. The first, from 1878-1880, and the second, from 1880-1882. In 1896 he was serving as a mission in Montana.

Matthias F. Cowley was called to the Apostleship in the October of 1897.

Matthias F. Cowley died June 1940.
