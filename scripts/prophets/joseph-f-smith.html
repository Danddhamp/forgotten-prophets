Joseph F. Smith

1838-1918

Ordained Counselor in the First Presidency: July 1, 1866

Apostle in the Quorum of the Twelve: August 29, 1877

Ordained Second Counselor in the First Presidency: October 10, 1880

Apostle in the Quorum of the Twelve: July 25, 1887

Ordained Second Counselor in the First Presidency: April 7, 1889

Ordained First Counselor in the First Presidency: October 6, 1901

President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles: October 10, 1901

President of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints: October 17, 1901

Biography

Joseph F. Smith died November 1918.
