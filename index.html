    "I tell you that if we do not feel in our hearts to sustain the prophets of God, we would not believe the Lord if He were to come Himself"
    -Abraham O. Woodruff, 1899 General Conference

    "when the servants of god are not bodily present with the people and their voices are not heard in their congregations, they begin to murmur and complain, but when the prophets are with them, the sheep soon recognize the voices of the shepherds and they are again established"
    -Abraham H. Cannon, 1890 General Conference

    "Some of the General Authorities [the Apostles] have had assigned to them a special calling; they possess a special gift; they are sustained as prophets, seers, and revelators, which gives them a special spiritual endowment in connection with their teaching of [this] people. They have the right, the power, and the authority to declare the mind and will of God to his people, subject to the overall power and authority of the President of the Church."
    -Boyd K. Packer (Quoting J. Reuben Clark Jr.) October 1996 General Conference
